# SIM-03 -- NICeMail, 06:14

**Act I · OSINT · Medium**

## The brief

A nightly firmware build fails at 06:14 every morning and nobody
reads the log. The commit address is anonymous but the author handle is not
anonymous - it is simply not a name. A hackathon photograph from three years
ago still carries its EXIF.

## What you must do

Identify the engineer whose checkout produced the CI log, read
the whiteboard from the photograph, and decode the receipt on the board.
