# SIM-13 -- Chitthi Is The Letter

**Act III · Crypto · Easy**

## The brief

A letter from inside the firmware office, forwarded by somebody who
should not have forwarded it. The letter is in a substitution cipher. The key
is not written down, because the man who wrote it works out of a document he
has to look at every day - and you have already read that document.

## What you must do

Derive the keyword from the SIM-02 nomenclature convention and
decrypt the line hidden in the letter.

## Getting started

Work through the `artifacts/` directory. Everything you need is there; nothing needs to be running.

```console
unzip vec-SIM-13-player.zip -d SIM-13
cd SIM-13/artifacts && ls
```
