COLLECTOR GRID -- FIELD REFERENCE
================================
Published with the advisory mirror for district collectors.

Each corroboration copy carries a collector grid: a fixed four column table
whose cells are filled column by column, top to bottom, starting at the first
column. A cell is either blank or holds a two digit decimal index.

Indices address the collector alphabet, 62 entries, index 0 first:

    ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZabcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789

Reading the grid column by column and skipping blank cells reproduces the
recorded line. The final column may be partly blank; that is normal and is
not an error in the copy.

The grid is a distribution aid. It is not encrypted.
