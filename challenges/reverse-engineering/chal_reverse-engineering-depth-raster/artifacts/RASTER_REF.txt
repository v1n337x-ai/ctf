RSTR RASTER REFERENCE
=====================
Published by the survey instrument vendor. Describes the encoding only.

Raster
------
The raster is 1 bit per pixel. It is stored run-length encoded as pairs of
(count, value) bytes, where count is 1..255 and value is 0 or 1.

The RLE encodes the raster in column-major order: column by column, top to
bottom, starting at the first column. To recover the row-major raster, expand
the RLE into a flat pixel array of width*height bits, then read it row by row.

Text
----
The raster renders text using the 5x7 bitmap font in FONT.txt. Each character
is 5 pixels wide and 7 pixels tall. Characters are separated by one blank
column. The text is rendered left to right, top to bottom.
