NOTES -- capture tooling (shipped in the mail thread)

The agreement came out of a mail gateway as a flattened PNG. Somebody
screenshotted a PDF, cropped it, and re-saved it so that the gateway's text
extractor would find nothing to scan. The redaction was applied before the
flatten, not after.

That is the detail worth sitting with. The redaction was applied BEFORE the
flatten.

Anyone who has run a document through an image pipeline will recognise what
that means. A redaction drawn in a vector document is a rectangle of pixels.
Once it is flattened, it is *still only* a rectangle of pixels, and a pixel
can carry more than its colour.

Conventions used by the tool (unversioned, standard):

  marker colour   the redaction bar is a solid fill of one RGB value; find
                  it by locating the solid block that is not paper and not a
                  rule line
  container       the low 2 bits of the BLUE channel of every pixel inside
                  the bar, in row-major order
  encoding        little-endian within each byte, 4 bytes per pixel,
                  most-significant group first
  key             each 2-bit group is XORed with 0x5A before use
  terminator      the payload ends at the first NUL group after valid data;
                  ignore padding

The bar's colour value, if you want to skip the hunt:

  bar RGB = (108, 108, 112)  (108, 108, 112)
  key     = 0x5A
  bits    = 2
