# SIM-04 -- Kavach Download

**Act I · OSINT · Medium**

## The brief

A paid-search advertiser has been buying the query 'kavach
download' for six capture windows. Every destination looks like a government
site. Nobody noticed because every one of them works.

## What you must do

Identify the destination present in every window that is not a
registered government domain, follow it to the dead-drop index, and decode the
receipt.
